Who We Are

Green Nepal is a dedicated environmental action and awareness platform built to preserve Nepal's rich landscapes, endemic wildlife, and vital river systems.

From the towering peaks of the Himalayas to the lush tropical forests of the Terai, Nepal encompasses some of planet Earth's most critical biodiversity hotspots. We work alongside local youth, community forest groups, and eco-activists to drive sustainable conservation.

Pillars of Action

Core focus areas guiding our conservation initiatives across the country.

Environmental Awareness

Educating youth and communities on eco-friendly practices, climate resilience, and sustainable living.

Forest Conservation

Supporting community forestry initiatives, reforestation projects, and watershed protection.

Wildlife Protection

Collaborating with local anti-poaching units to safeguard endangered species like Red Pandas and Tigers.

Our Vision

A sustainable future where Nepal's forests, mountains, rivers, and wildlife flourish in harmony with thriving, climate-resilient communities for generations to come.
