About Green Nepal

Protecting nature, preserving biodiversity, and creating a greener future.

Who We Are

Green Nepal is an environmental awareness platform dedicated to showcasing Nepal's natural beauty, biodiversity and conservation efforts.

From the Himalayas to the forests of Terai, Nepal contains some of the world's most valuable ecosystems. Our goal is to educate people and encourage protection of nature.
