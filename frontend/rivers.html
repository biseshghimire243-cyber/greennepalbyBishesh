Eastern Nepal

Koshi River

Known as the "Sorrow of Bihar" due to flooding, the Koshi is Nepal's largest river system, formed by seven major tributaries in the High Himalayas.

  • Length: ~720 km
  • Origin: Mt. Everest Region
  • Highlight: Koshi Tappu Wildlife Sanctuary
Central Nepal

Gandaki River

Carving the deepest gorge in the world (Kali Gandaki Gorge), this historic river system feeds central Nepal and is famous for sacred Shaligram stones.

  • Length: ~630 km
  • Origin: Mustang & Annapurna
  • Highlight: World-class White Water Rafting
Western Nepal

Karnali River

Nepal's longest river, flowing through wild and remote western landscapes. It remains one of Nepal's last remaining free-flowing rivers.

  • Length: ~507 km (in Nepal)
  • Origin: Mt. Kailash (Tibet)
  • Highlight: Endangered Gangetic Dolphins
