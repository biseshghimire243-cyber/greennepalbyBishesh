UNESCO Site

Chitwan National Park

Nepal's first national park, established in 1973. Celebrated globally for protecting the endangered Greater One-horned Rhino and Royal Bengal Tiger.

  • Area: 952.6 km²
  • Location: Southern Terai Lowlands
  • Key Wildlife: Rhinos, Tigers, Gharials
UNESCO Site

Sagarmatha National Park

Home to Mount Everest and dramatic high-altitude Himalayan terrains, deep gorges, glaciers, and iconic Sherpa cultural sites.

  • Area: 1,148 km²
  • Location: Solukhumbu District
  • Key Wildlife: Snow Leopards, Musk Deer
Lowland Reserve

Bardia National Park

The largest untouched wilderness area in Nepal's Terai region, offering prime territory for spotting wild elephants and tigers.

  • Area: 968 km²
  • Location: Western Nepal
  • Key Wildlife: Wild Elephants, Tigers
Alpine Park

Langtang National Park

Stretching from oak and pine forests to alpine meadows and glaciers, famous for its high biodiversity and cultural Himalayan trails.

  • Area: 1,710 km²
  • Location: Central Himalayas
  • Key Wildlife: Red Pandas, Black Bears
Mountain Reserve

Rara National Park

Nepal's smallest national park, protecting pristine coniferous forests and Rara Lake—the largest freshwater lake in the country.

  • Area: 106 km²
  • Location: Mugu & Jumla
  • Key Wildlife: Red Pandas, Himalayan Monals
Valley Watershed

Shivapuri Nagarjun Park

Located on the northern fringe of Kathmandu Valley, serving as a primary water catchment area and a haven for forest birds.

  • Area: 159 km²
  • Location: Kathmandu Region
  • Key Wildlife: Leopards, Wild Boars, Birds
