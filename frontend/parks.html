National Parks of Nepal

Explore Nepal's beautiful protected areas.

Explore Parks

Discover forests, mountains and wildlife habitats.

Chitwan National Park

Nepal's first national park and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, famous for the one-horned rhinoceros, Bengal tiger and rich biodiversity.

Sagarmatha National Park

Home to Mount Everest and spectacular Himalayan scenery with glaciers, Sherpa villages and unique alpine wildlife.

Bardia National Park

Nepal's largest lowland national park, known for Bengal tigers, wild elephants, crocodiles and untouched forests.

Langtang National Park

Famous for snow-capped mountains, red pandas, Himalayan black bears and beautiful trekking routes.

Rara National Park

Home to the stunning Rara Lake, surrounded by pine forests and breathtaking mountain landscapes.

Shivapuri National Park

Located near Kathmandu, this park protects forests, wildlife and one of the valley's most important water sources.
