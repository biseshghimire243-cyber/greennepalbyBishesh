Iconic Species

Nepal is home to incredible terrestrial and avian biodiversity across diverse climates.

Vulnerable

One-horned Rhinoceros

Nepal is globally celebrated for successful conservation drives that brought the Greater One-horned Rhinoceros back from the brink of extinction.

  • Habitat: Floodplain Grasslands
  • Region: Chitwan & Bardia
  • Protection: Zero-Poaching Zones
Endangered

Royal Bengal Tiger

Nepal became the first country in the world to double its wild tiger population, protecting core jungle territories through community patrols.

  • Habitat: Subtropical Terai Forests
  • Region: Terai Arc Landscape
  • Protection: Wildlife Corridors
Endangered

Red Panda

The elusive Red Panda inhabits cool, temperate bamboo forests in the Himalayan foothills, protected by indigenous forest guardians.

  • Habitat: Temperate Bamboo Canopy
  • Region: Langtang & Makalu Barun
  • Protection: Community Forests
