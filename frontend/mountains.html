Sagarmatha Region

Mount Everest

Known as Sagarmatha in Nepal, Mount Everest stands as the roof of the world and the ultimate symbol of Himalayan splendor.

  • Elevation: 8,848.86 m
  • Location: Solukhumbu
  • Highlight: Sagarmatha National Park
Annapurna Region

Annapurna I

A breathtaking massif in central Nepal known worldwide for the iconic Annapurna Circuit and rich alpine biodiversity.

  • Elevation: 8,091 m
  • Location: Gandaki Province
  • Highlight: World's Top Trekking Trail
Gorkha Region

Mount Manaslu

Known as the "Mountain of the Spirit", Manaslu is the 8th highest mountain in the world, offering remote alpine vistas.

  • Elevation: 8,163 m
  • Location: West-Central Nepal
  • Highlight: Manaslu Conservation Area
